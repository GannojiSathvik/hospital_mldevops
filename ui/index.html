Data drift check

Compares recent prediction inputs (data/inference/inference_log.csv) against the training data with Evidently. Drift means live patients no longer look like the patients the model learned from. Retraining is recommended when more than 30% of features drift.

Who can do what (RBAC)

Mirrors configs/access_control.yaml. The server enforces it on every request: no key or a wrong key gives 401 (we don't know who you are); a valid key without the permission gives 403 (we know who you are, but you may not do this).
